# RAG exchange

## Query and model

what products of and of what industries are shortlisted for the extension of the CBAM?
gemini:gemini-2.5-flash
## Answer

The following products and industries are shortlisted or identified for further consideration for the extension of the CBAM:

*   **Non-ferrous metals**:
    *   Lead (HS codes 7801, 7804, 7806) (Source: CBAM horizontal extension Final Report _CLEAN_31.10.2025.pdf, page 56, chunk 117)
    *   Zinc (HS codes 7901, 7903, 7904, 7905, 7907) (Source: CBAM horizontal extension Final Report _CLEAN_31.10.2025.pdf, page 56, chunk 117)
    *   Copper (HS Chapter 74), if the CBAM is extended to lead and zinc. (Source: CBAM horizontal extension Final Report _CLEAN_31.10.2025.pdf, page 56, chunk 118)
    *   "Articles of ...n.e.s." groups, like HS 7419 (other articles of copper, n.e.s.), are generally excluded, especially because they include downstream products not produced in EU ETS installations. (Source: CBAM horizontal extension Final Report _CLEAN_31.10.2025.pdf, page 114, chunk 241)
*   **Mineral oil refinery products**: All goods produced by the refinery sector, including liquid fuels, petroleum coke (petcoke), inputs to the chemical industry (naphtha, pyrolysis gas (pygas), aromatics etc.), and speciality products like lubricants, waxes, and bitumens. Alternatively, the scope could theoretically be limited to:
    *   Only fractions used in the chemical industry.
    *   Only fuels.
    *   Any other combination of selection criteria. (Source: CBAM horizontal extension Final Report _CLEAN_31.10.2025.pdf, page 114, chunk 241)
*   **Paper and paperboard**: All CN codes under HS Chapter 48, if the main precursor HS Chapter 47 (wood pulp) is included in the CBAM. (Source: CBAM horizontal extension Final Report _CLEAN_31.10.2025.pdf, page 56, chunk 118)
*   **Bituminous mixtures**: CN codes under HS 2715, if the CBAM is extended to CN codes under HS 2713 (which includes petroleum bitumen). (Source: CBAM horizontal extension Final Report _CLEAN_31.10.2025.pdf, page 56, chunk 118)
*   **Organic chemicals**:
    *   CN codes under HS 2901 (specifically high purity ethylene, propylene, butylene, and butadiene), if the CBAM is extended to CN 27111400 (which covers lower purity ethylene, propylene, butylene, and butadiene). (Source: CBAM horizontal extension Final Report _CLEAN_31.10.2025.pdf, page 56, chunk 118)
    *   Methanol, polyethylene, polypropylene, PET, styrene, and PVC are noted as organic chemicals with significant embedded emissions that are included in the initial Task 1 shortlist. (Source: CBAM horizontal extension Final Report _CLEAN_31.10.2025.pdf, page 57, chunk 119)
*   **Glass bottles and containers**: All CN codes under HS 701090, should the CBAM be extended to glass bottles and containers that are on the final shortlist for further consideration. (Source: CBAM horizontal extension Final Report _CLEAN_31.10.2025.pdf, page 56, chunk 118)
*   **Downstream products of glass fibres**: Should the CBAM be extended to glass fibres, with downstream products identified on a product-by-product basis. (Source: CBAM horizontal extension Final Report _CLEAN_31.10.2025.pdf, page 56, chunk 118)
*   **Ferro-alloys**: All ferro-alloys that may contain silicon or even all CN codes under the HS 4-digit code for ferro-alloys (HS 7202), should the CBAM be extended to ferro-silicon and ferro-silico-manganese. (Source: CBAM horizontal extension Final Report _CLEAN_31.10.2025.pdf, page 56, chunk 118)
*   **(Pre-consumer) scrap**: General consideration for inclusion, particularly for aluminium, iron, and steel where scrap can be considered a precursor. If included for these, then scrap of other relevant sectors (glass, paper, and polymers) may also need consideration. (Source: CBAM horizontal extension Final Report _CLEAN_31.10.2025.pdf, page 56, chunk 117)
