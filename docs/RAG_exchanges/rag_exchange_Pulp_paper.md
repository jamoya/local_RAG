## Answer

The shortlisted products from the pulp and paper industry for the extension of the CBAM are divided into three broad categories:

*   **Chemical wood pulp:** all CN codes under HS 4702 and 4703 (Source: CBAM horizontal extension Final Report _CLEAN_31.10.2025.pdf, page 38).
*   **Mechanical wood pulp:** all CN codes under HS 4701 and 4705, 4706 (Source: CBAM horizontal extension Final Report _CLEAN_31.10.2025.pdf, page 38).
*   **Paper and paperboard:**
    *   At the HS 4-digit level, all CN codes under HS 4801 (Source: CBAM horizontal extension Final Report _CLEAN_31.10.2025.pdf, page 38).
    *   At the HS 6-digit level, all CN codes under HS 480256, 480257, 480261, 480262, 480269, 480300, 480411, 481159 and 481190 (Source: CBAM horizontal extension Final Report _CLEAN_31.10.2025.pdf, page 38).
