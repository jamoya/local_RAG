## Query and model

what industries and products are sort listed for the scope extension in the CBAM?
gemini:gemini-2.5-flash


## Answer

The following industries and products are shortlisted or identified for further consideration for the scope extension in the CBAM:

*   **Goods at risk of carbon leakage other than those listed in Annex I of the CBAM Regulation**:
    *   Organic chemicals and polymers (Source: CBAM horizontal extension Final Report \_CLEAN\_31.10.2025.pdf, page 23).
*   **Input materials (precursor goods)** for the goods listed in Annex I of the CBAM Regulation (Source: CBAM horizontal extension Final Report \_CLEAN\_31.10.2025.pdf, page 23).
*   **Embedded emissions in the transport** of the goods listed in Annex I of the CBAM Regulation, and of new goods with potential for CBAM scope extension (Source: CBAM horizontal extension Final Report \_CLEAN\_31.10.2025.pdf, page 23).
*   **Aluminium**:
    *   Aluminium oxide (Source: CBAM horizontal extension Final Report \_CLEAN\_31.10.2025.pdf, page 54).
    *   CN 28183000 aluminium hydroxide (shortlisted under inorganic chemicals, also analysed as an aluminium precursor) (Source: CBAM horizontal extension Final Report \_CLEAN\_31.10.2025.pdf, page 54, 56).
*   **Other non-ferrous metals**:
    *   **Unwrought lead**: CN codes under HS 780110 and 780199 (fully shortlisted) (Source: CBAM horizontal extension Final Report \_CLEAN\_31.10.2025.pdf, page 54).
        *   Consideration for extension to all CN codes under HS 7801 (Unwrought lead) (Source: CBAM horizontal extension Final Report \_CLEAN\_31.10.2025.pdf, page 54, 56).
        *   Consideration for extension to CN codes under HS 7804 (lead plates, sheets, strip and foil; lead powders and flakes) and 7806 (other articles of lead) (Source: CBAM horizontal extension Final Report \_CLEAN\_31.10.2025.pdf, page 54, 56).
    *   **Unwrought zinc**: CN codes under HS 790111 and 790112 (fully shortlisted) (Source: CBAM horizontal extension Final Report \_CLEAN\_31.10.2025.pdf, page 54).
        *   Consideration for extension to all CN codes under HS 7901 (Unwrought zinc) (Source: CBAM horizontal extension Final Report \_CLEAN\_31.10.2025.pdf, page 54, 56).
        *   Consideration for extension to CN codes under HS 7903 (lead dust, powders and flakes), 7904 (zinc bars, rods, profiles and wires), 7905 (zinc plates, sheets, strip and), and 7907 (Source: CBAM horizontal extension Final Report \_CLEAN\_31.10.2025.pdf, page 54, 56).
    *   Inclusion of **copper** (HS Chapter 74), if the CBAM is extended to lead and zinc (Source: CBAM horizontal extension Final Report \_CLEAN\_31.10.2025.pdf, page 56).
*   **Scrap**:
    *   (Pre-consumer) scrap in general, with specific mention of aluminium, iron, and steel (pre-consumer) scraps. If included, then scrap of other relevant sectors (glass, paper, and polymers) may also have to be considered (Source: CBAM horizontal extension Final Report \_CLEAN\_31.10.2025.pdf, page 56).
*   **Paper and Paperboard**:
    *   All CN codes under HS Chapter 48 (paper and paperboard), should the main precursor HS Chapter 47 (wood pulp) be included in the CBAM (Source: CBAM horizontal extension Final Report \_CLEAN\_31.10.2025.pdf, page 56).
*   **Bituminous mixtures**:
    *   CN codes under HS 2715 (bituminous mixtures), if the CBAM is extended to CN codes under HS 2713 (which includes petroleum bitumen) (Source: CBAM horizontal extension Final Report \_CLEAN\_31.10.2025.pdf, page 56).
*   **Organic Chemicals**:
    *   CN codes under HS 2901 (specifically high purity ethylene, propylene, butylene, and butadiene), if the CBAM is extended to CN 27111400 (which covers lower purity ethylene, propylene, butylene, and butadiene) (Source: CBAM horizontal extension Final Report \_CLEAN\_31.10.2025.pdf, page 56).
*   **Glass**:
    *   CN codes of all glass bottles and containers under HS 701090, should the CBAM be extended to glass bottles and containers that are on the final shortlist for further consideration (Source: CBAM horizontal extension Final Report \_CLEAN\_31.10.2025.pdf, page 56).
    *   Downstream products of glass fibres, should the CBAM be extended to glass fibres (Source: CBAM horizontal extension Final Report \_CLEAN\_31.10.2025.pdf, page 56).
*   **Ferro-alloys**:
    *   All ferro-alloys that may contain silicon or even to all CN codes under the HS 4-digit code for ferro-alloys (HS 7202), should the CBAM be extended to ferro-silicon and ferro-silico-manganese (Source: CBAM horizontal extension Final Report \_CLEAN\_31.10.2025.pdf, page 56).
*   **Refinery products**:
    *   All refinery products, including liquid fuels, petroleum coke (petcoke), inputs to the chemical industry (naphtha, pyrolysis gas (pygas), aromatics etc.), and speciality products like lubricants, waxes, bitumens. The simplest approach would be to cover all goods produced by the refinery sector (Source: CBAM horizontal extension Final Report \_CLEAN\_31.10.2025.pdf, page 114).
