what products of the pulp and paper industry are shortlisted for an extension of the CBAM?

## Answer

The products of the pulp and paper industry shortlisted for an extension of the CBAM are:

- **Chemical wood pulp**: all CN codes under HS 4702 and 4703
- **Mechanical wood pulp**: all CN codes under HS 4701 and 4705, 4706
- **Paper and paperboard**:
  - At the HS 4-digit level: all CN codes under HS 4801
  - At the HS 6-digit level: all CN codes under HS 480256, 480257, 480261, 480262, 480269, 480300, 480411, 481159, and 481190

For wood pulp (HS Chapter 47) at a group level, only CN codes under HS 4704 (Chemical wood pulp, sulphite, other than dissolving grades - unbleached: coniferous) are not shortlisted, representing 0.5% of the import value and weight of HS Chapter 47 (Source: CBAM horizontal extension Final Report _CLEAN_31.10.2025.pdf, page 38).
