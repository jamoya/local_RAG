## Answer

The shortlisted products of the chemical industry for inclusion in a CBAM scope extension include:

- Organic chemicals known to have significant embedded emissions such as:
  - Methanol
  - Polyethylene
  - Polypropylene
  - PET (Polyethylene terephthalate)
  - Styrene
  - PVC (Polyvinyl chloride)

- Inorganic chemicals and polymers identified through a systematic assessment of value chains (specific substances not fully listed in the excerpt but referenced in section 3.5).

- Chemicals and polymers identified as candidates after examining:
  - The chemical industry overview (section 3.1)
  - Use of EU ETS data to identify important chemicals (section 3.2)
  - Main value chains of large-scale chemicals from common raw materials (section 3.3)
  - Criteria for choosing chemicals (section 3.4)

- Olefins and aromatics as main targets of horizontal CBAM extension, including:
  - Ethylene
  - Propylene
  - Butylene
  - Butadiene
  - Aromatics (general category)

- Chemicals already in the existing CBAM scope considered at the beginning of the value chain:
  - Hydrogen
  - Ammonia
  - Nitric acid
  - Urea

- Precursors identified as possible candidates for inclusion:
  - Lime
  - Dolime
  - Magnesia
  - Alumina
  - Coke (with special consideration for co-products and emission attribution)

- Potential extensions to related CN codes under certain HS chapters if main precursors are included:
  - All CN codes under HS Chapter 48 (paper and paperboard) if HS Chapter 47 (wood pulp) is included
  - CN codes under HS 2715 (bituminous mixtures) if HS 2713 (petroleum bitumen) is included
  - CN codes under HS 2901 (high purity ethylene, propylene, butylene, butadiene) if CN 27111400 (lower purity versions) is included
  - CN codes of all glass bottles and containers under HS 701090 if glass bottles and containers are included
  - Downstream products of glass fibres if glass fibres are included
  - All ferro-alloys under HS 7202 if ferro-silicon and ferro-silico-manganese are included
  - Copper (HS Chapter 74) if lead and zinc are included

These shortlisted chemicals and polymers form the basis for assessing technical feasibility for inclusion in the CBAM under Task 2.3 (section 4.4).
